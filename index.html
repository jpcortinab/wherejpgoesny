How this happened

From a spreadsheet to something people actually use

I used to respond to "where should I eat?" with a few names off the top of my head. But the right recommendation depends on much more than that — neighborhood, cuisine, budget, occasion, and what kind of experience someone's after.

I'd already been keeping a personal spreadsheet of everywhere I'd tried, so I started organizing it around those questions and sharing tailored recommendations instead. As more people used it, I kept refining the list based on what was actually useful to them, and eventually turned it into a website they could navigate on their own.

What started as a way to keep track of a personal interest became a way to share a part of New York I love. Something I started for fun is now something friends and family actually use — and now, so can you.

How to read the ratings

The scale

★★★★★Good — would return, but wouldn't make a special trip
★★★★★Fair — only if invited, probably wouldn't recommend
